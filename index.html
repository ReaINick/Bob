HEROES DESERVE SUPPORT WHEN DUTY CALLS

Superheroes should have access to funding when the reason is valid

EVIDENCE FROM THE FIELD

"He puts papers in his briefcase and drives away"

This line can be interpreted as Superman working a job before leaving said job, showing how a superhero like Superman could be deserving a payment

"I watch superman fly away today"

This notes the flip side of Superman's activities, which are different from a normal form of work

"I've got a busy day today"

This connects with the previous quote, which solidifies the work Superman partakes in outside the scope of a normal job

WHY HEROES NEED SUPPORT

Superman works a normal job

This is reference to his actual work he partakes in, which he likely gets pay for

Superman works outside of the scope of what defines a normal job

This is in reference to Superman's duties that he places upon himself outside the scope of the working world, he defines this by his own terms and avoids government intervention

Superman provides a service which cannot be easily quantified in monetary value

Superman's hero work is beyond normal calculations for regular compensation, saving a life has infinite value above working a job in terms of pay alone.

ADDRESSING THE OPPOSITION

Superhero's do this for the greater good, which in of itself is a form of compensation

Superman may do his work for the greater good, but this alone shouldn't exclude him from support

Superheroes are already given generous amounts of objects and commemorative goods by the people they save

Our Response:

While this may be true, a commemorative good may not be sufficient to continue to save the city, money is expendable.

IF superheroes are not heroes, they become villians and kill everyone, so we should

Money is an object of corruption, however if money is used to not constitute power, and the hero such as superman already has enough power to do as he pleases, power from money is but a perk that has little meaning, and therefore little effect.
